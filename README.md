# You Will Never Leave IKEA

A short first-person game about a Saturday at a flat-pack megastore. You only need a lamp. The goal is to get out.

**Play:** open `index.html` in a desktop browser (or the GitHub Pages link for this repo).

## Controls

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| W A S D | walk |
| Mouse | look around (click to capture the mouse) |
| Shift | hurry (costs patience) |
| Space | jump (try the beds) |
| E or left click | take · sit · open · read |
| Q | your shopping list |
| M | sound on/off |

## What's in the store

- A showroom in seven departments: living rooms, Sofa land, walk-through small spaces, kitchens, a dark lighting department full of glowing lamps, a mattress field and a children's play area, with arrows on the floor and shortcuts that may not be shortcuts
- Faceless mannequin shoppers who browse, bicker and occasionally stare
- Drawers and wardrobes to open, lamps to switch off, five Allen keys to find
- Little Emil, hiding somewhere, and his worried parents
- A restaurant with meatballs, a serving line and windows onto the car park
- Beds you can bounce on, a ball pit, a shop cat called Sofa, and flash sales you can race other shoppers to
- Music that changes by department, and a receipt that lists what kind of shopper you were today
- A dark self-serve warehouse with an as-is corner and a forklift, and six endings

Built with [Three.js](https://threejs.org) r160. Everything (furniture, textures, signage, music) is generated in code; there are no asset files.

Unofficial fan parody. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Inter IKEA Systems B.V.
